I build software for people the tech industry usually skips: students in Afghanistan preparing for the
exam that decides their future, small offices with no security team, and buyers and sellers across
Uzbekistan. Most of my users speak Dari, Pashto or Uzbek, many are on a cheap Android phone, and the internet
isn't always there. Those limits shape how I build.
